About me

I'm Dale Cyrus D. Edrosalan, a third-year BSCS student at Gordon College in Olongapo City, Zambales.

I keep a Dean's List standing and I'm an OWWA EDSP scholar, so I take my studies seriously and like to see things through.

I enjoy both sides of building software: designing how something looks and feels, then writing the code that makes it work.

Outside of class I play Roblox, Valorant, and Mobile Legends, and I watch anime. Gaming is where a lot of my side projects start, from Roblox scripts to Blender models.

What I do

  • Apps and websites: I build mobile and web apps end to end, from the database to the screens people tap.
  • Games: I script NPC and boss AI pathfinding for Roblox games in Lua.
  • 3D and graphics: I model characters, weapons, and structures in Blender and SketchUp, and design graphics in Illustrator, Figma, and Canva.
  • Hardware and networks: I repair PCs and laptops, set up networks, and hold TESDA NC II in Computer Systems Servicing.
